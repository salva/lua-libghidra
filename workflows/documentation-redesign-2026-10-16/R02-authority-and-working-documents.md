# R02 — Audience, current authority and working-document separation

Category: authority/organization; cross-cutting. Final docs need current task and
reference content, not development evidence. [Developer index](../../docs/development/README.md#L13)
still mixes chronological correction claims with instructions; plans/reviews are
under the same docs hierarchy. Old location or previous acceptance is not a reason
to preserve that structure. Required exact notices are not optional history.

Working documents must be separate from the main user/contributor documentation,
installed manual and site. Determine deliberate disposition of existing reports
and current contracts without deleting Git/runtime history or blindly breaking
live workflow ownership/reference dependencies. No redirect scaffolding by default.
